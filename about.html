Founder & Lead Consultant

Mahesh Krishnan brings business experience into the learning room.

A leadership career spanning more than three decades, a decade-plus in training and development, and an academic grounding in psychology and learning.

A practical, human-centered approach

Learning that respects the complexity of work.

Mahesh has held leadership roles across business development, sales and marketing, brand communications and new business start-ups. For over a decade, he has worked in training and development, building Skillhone Academy as a consultancy for training, mentoring, coaching and consulting engagements.

His work draws on a continuing interest in human psychology and on experience with the challenges organizations and participants bring into a program. He uses discussion, relevant activities, role-play and reflection to make learning active and connected to everyday work.

His areas of focus include behavioral transformation for effective leadership, communication, corporate storytelling, mentoring, design thinking and entrepreneurship.

How learning is approached

Practice, reflect, apply.

Programs are designed to move between useful ideas and participants’ own situations.

Understand the need

Start with the business context, audience, current challenges and intended outcomes.

Make it relevant

Use examples and activities that connect the topic to participant roles and experience.

Learn by doing

Facilitate practice through conversation, role-play, group work and reflection.

Carry it into work

Agree what participants should take forward and how the organization wants to review progress.
